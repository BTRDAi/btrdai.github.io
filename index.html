Common questions

Working with BTRDAi.

Does BTRDAi take on website projects?

Website design and development is an area we can scope with organizations. We start with an inquiry to determine requirements, delivery approach, and whether specialist support is needed.

Can a redesigned site connect to an existing business platform?

Often yes. Links to approved third-party portals can be straightforward; embedded experiences, login integrations, and data exchange require technical and security review. We assess the specific provider and scope before committing.

Can BTRDAi build healthcare or other sensitive-data systems?

Regulated data projects must be evaluated individually. They can require specialized partners, contractual safeguards, and a separate security/compliance plan. A standard website project does not include storing sensitive patient data.

How do we request an estimate?

Use the contact link below and tell us about your organization, project goals, required features, and target timing. We can then discuss a realistic scope and estimate.
