Featured Product

GameOn: AI Sports Intelligence

GameOn is BTRDAi's sports intelligence platform. It brings live player prop markets, player trends, historical performance, AI-powered signals, and model analytics together in one streamlined experience.

Live Props Current player prop markets and data-driven signals.
AI Lineup Builder Selections that meet stricter confidence and playability criteria.
Hot Picks Current market context combined with recent player trends.
Player History Recent completed-game performance and market comparisons.
Analytics Model performance and previously graded AI Builder results.
Responsible Information Sports intelligence and analytical information without guaranteed outcomes.
